# TaxiScope NYC

TaxiScope NYC helps New York City riders explore historical taxi demand, fares, and trip durations by zone and time. It uses NYC Taxi and Limousine Commission trip records to help riders compare travel windows and make informed trip decisions.

## Project Proposal

TaxiScope NYC is an interactive map using TLC trip records to show historical trip volume, typical duration, fare ranges, and better or worse travel times by zone. Comparing taxi demand with typical Uber/Lyft surge periods is a stretch goal.

## Product Requirements PRD

The PRD defines three rider personas—daily commuter, budget-conscious visitor, and taxi-vs-rideshare decider—plus an administrator. The administrator manages data quality and community safety.

**MVP:** A taxi-zone map, pickup/destination selection, demand heatmap, day/hour filters, fare ranges, trip durations, best/worst travel windows, and ride-share posts.

**MLP:** Authentication, nearby cheaper-zone suggestions, saved routes, a “leave by” planner, side-by-side zone comparisons, and user-submitted fare reports.

Historical data represents past patterns; it is not a live fare quote or a guarantee of future trip conditions.

## Epic Breakdown

The product capabilities are organized into five epics:

1. **Explore Zone Demand & Fares** — browse historical zone demand, fare ranges, trip durations, and travel windows.
2. **Personalize & Plan Trips** — save routes and plan trips around preferred arrival times.
3. **Compare Taxi vs. Rideshare** — compare options and contribute fare reports.
4. **Ride-Sharing & Messaging** — find riders with similar routes and coordinate trips.
5. **Admin & Data Management** — maintain data quality, zone information, and community moderation.

The core exploration experience provides historical zone and time patterns. Personal planning, taxi-versus-rideshare comparisons, ride-sharing, and data administration extend that foundation.
