# TaxiScope NYC

TaxiScope NYC helps NYC riders explore historical taxi demand, fares, and trip durations by zone and time.

## Product Overview

Users can select pickup and destination zones, filter by day and hour, and compare historical travel patterns to help plan a trip. The project uses NYC TLC trip data.

## Personas

- **Alex — Daily Commuter:** compares time and cost for regular trips.
- **Priya — Budget-Conscious Visitor:** looks for affordable travel windows.
- **Marcus — Taxi-vs-Rideshare Decider:** compares taxi and rideshare options.
- **Administrator:** manages data quality and community content.

## Epics

1. **Explore Zone Demand & Fares**
2. **Personalize & Plan Trips**
3. **Compare Taxi vs. Rideshare**
4. **Ride-Sharing & Messaging**
5. **Admin & Data Management**
