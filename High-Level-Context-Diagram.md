## Epic-to-Persona Mapping

| Epic | Personas | What they use the epic for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. Explore Zone Demand & Fares** | Alex, Priya, Marcus | Explore taxi zones and historical demand, fare ranges, trip duration, and better/worse travel windows. This epic is also open to any visitor. |
| **2. Personalize & Plan Trips** | Alex, Priya | Sign in, save regular routes, plan around an arrival time, and find potentially better nearby pickup zones. |
| **3. Compare Taxi vs. Rideshare** | Marcus | Compare taxi and rideshare patterns and submit observed fare/quote reports. |
| **4. Ride-Sharing & Messaging** | Alex, Priya | Create or find ride-share posts, indicate interest, and coordinate with other riders. |
| **5. Admin & Data Management** | Admin | Import and aggregate data, manage zone metadata and cleaning rules, and moderate reports or ride-share content. |
